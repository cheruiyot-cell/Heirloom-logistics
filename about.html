OUR STORY

From Personal Frustration to Professional Mission

Heirloom Logistics was not born in a boardroom. It was born out of a frustrating moving day experience that felt all too familiar.

Our founder, Brian Mutiso, watched a close friend lose a brand-new TV and a glass dining table to a moving crew that simply didn't care. The crew arrived late, packed the truck carelessly, and when the damage was discovered, they vanished.

Brian realized that the moving industry in Kenya had a trust problem. There was no company that offered professional care at a fair price.

So, he decided to build one.

"We treat every sofa like a leather couch and every plate like fine china. Not because they are expensive, but because they are yours." — Brian Mutiso, Founder
