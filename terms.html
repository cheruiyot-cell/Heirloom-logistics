Terms of Service

Last updated: January 2026

1. Quotes & Estimates

Quotes issued by Heirloom Logistics are based on the volume, distance, and access details provided at the time of the video survey or written description. Quotes are binding unless the actual inventory materially differs from what was described.

If you add significant items after the quote has been issued, we reserve the right to issue a revised quote.

2. Booking & Deposit

To confirm a move date, a deposit is required as agreed at the time of booking. The balance is payable upon delivery of the goods at the destination. We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and cash.

3. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Free rescheduling with 48 hours notice.
  • Full deposit refund on cancellation 72+ hours before the move.
  • Cancellations within 72 hours may forfeit the deposit.

4. Items We Do Not Move

We do not transport: hazardous materials (petrol, propane, paint thinners, fireworks), perishable food, live animals, and firearms. For a full list, see our Packing Guide.

5. Damage & Liability

We handle every item with professional care. If an item is damaged due to our negligence, we will repair or compensate you. Damage must be reported at the time of delivery, before our crew leaves the destination.

We are not liable for damage to items that were inadequately packed by the client, or for internal mechanical failure of electronics that show no external damage.

6. Insurance

Goods in Transit insurance is available on request. Please notify us before your move if you require coverage so we can include the cost in your quote.

7. Access & Parking

You are responsible for ensuring safe access at both pickup and delivery locations, including any necessary permissions from estate management or landlords. Where lifts are unavailable, our crew will carry items via stairs at no additional charge.

8. Time Estimates

Time estimates provided are approximate. Nairobi traffic, estate gate delays, and other factors outside our control may affect timing. We will communicate proactively if we anticipate delays.

9. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of Nairobi.
