The Heirloom Standard

Three commitments. Zero compromise. This is what every single move with us looks like.

Moving Is Broken in Kenya. Here's How We Fix It.

Too many movers show up late, handle your things carelessly, and change the price after the job. We saw the problem and built a company around solving it.

The Heirloom Standard is our public promise. It is what separates us from the casual laborer with a pickup truck. Every crew member is trained on it. Every client is protected by it.

The Three Pillars

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every single move—residential, commercial, or single-item.

⏰

Radical Reliability

We arrive within the time window we promise. We plan for Nairobi traffic, estate gate delays, and lift access. If we are running behind, we call you before the window closes. No excuses, no ghosting.

🤲

Obsessive Care

We treat your belongings the way we would want our own treated. We bring professional packing materials and use them generously. We wrap, we pad, we strap. Nothing shifts in transit. We don't cut corners, even when no one is watching.

🤝

Transparent Integrity

The price we agree on is the price you pay. If we make a mistake, we own it. If we break something, we fix it or pay for it. Full stop. No hidden fees, no surprise charges, no vanishing after the job.

What Every Heirloom Move Looks Like

01

Pre-Move Planning

We conduct a video survey of your home, assess the volume, review access at both locations, and send you a fixed, binding quote in writing. No guesswork, no "we'll see when we get there."

02

Professional Packing

Our trained crew arrives on time with bubble wrap, moving blankets, industrial tape, and labeled boxes. Every fragile item is wrapped. Every box is labeled by room. Every piece of furniture is protected.

03

Strategic Loading

We load heavy items at the bottom, fragile items on top, and everything is strapped to prevent shifting. Our trucks are covered, GPS-tracked, and driven by permanent staff—not roadside casuals.

04

Careful Unloading & Placement

We place items where you want them in your new home. We reassemble furniture. We unwrap everything. And we don't leave until you have walked through and confirmed you are satisfied.

05

Debris Sweep & Close-Out

We take all packing materials, tape, and boxes away with us. Your new home is left clean and ready to live in. If anything was damaged, we address it before we leave the property.
