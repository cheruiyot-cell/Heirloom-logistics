Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Heirloom Logistics ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a quote request through our contact form, which opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing your name, email, phone number, moving addresses, and item descriptions.
  • Contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Submit a video survey of your items for assessment.

We do not collect payment card information through this website. Payments are handled separately via M-Pesa or bank transfer.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Prepare and send you a moving quote.
  • Coordinate and deliver the moving service you requested.
  • Communicate with you about your move (confirmations, updates, follow-ups).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. When you submit our quote form, the information you enter is used only to build a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your own device. The message is sent from your WhatsApp account to ours. We do not transmit your data to any other third party.

  • WhatsApp / Meta — when you submit the quote form, your message is delivered through WhatsApp. Your use of WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. If you prefer not to use WhatsApp, please call us on 0702 555 093 instead.
  • Law enforcement — only where legally required.

Note: This website does not operate a backend form-processing service. There is no third-party form handler receiving your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain quote requests and moving records for up to 24 months for accounting, insurance, and dispute-resolution purposes. After that period, records are deleted or anonymised.

6. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

7. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not set first-party tracking cookies. If analytics tools (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) are enabled on this site, they may set cookies to measure traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, restrict access to your data to authorised personnel, and store records on access-controlled devices. No online transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
